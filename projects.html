The Obsidian Penthouse

A 6,200 sq.ft double-height penthouse commission in Beverly Hills. The brief required transforming a concrete high-rise shell into a warm, monolithic residential sanctuary that maximizes skyline vistas while retaining intimate acoustic quiet.

6,200 Sq.Ft Residence Completion: 2025 Scope: Turnkey Architecture Nero Marquina • Smoked Walnut

Key interventions included a sunken conversation lounge, floor-to-ceiling bookmatched Italian marble fireplace, motorized fluted acoustic panels, and automated circadian lighting scenes.
