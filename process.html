THE 4-PHASE LIFECYCLE

The Structured Architectural Journey

Examine the exact timeline, client deliverables, and milestones of our turnkey practice.

01
PHASE 01 • WEEKS 1 TO 2 Client Milestone: Design Brief Sign-off

Discover • Lifestyle Immersion & Laser Survey

We conduct a millimeter-accurate 3D laser scan of your property while facilitating a private lifestyle interview to audit daily living habits, entertaining flow, acoustic sensitivities, and aesthetic aspirations.

Deliverables: 3D As-Built CAD Scan • Spatial Feasibility Study • Moodboard Concept
02
PHASE 02 • WEEKS 3 TO 5 Client Milestone: 3D Visual Rendering Approval

Design • Schematics & 4K Photorealistic Digital Twins

Our 3D visualization artists build virtual digital twins of your space. You preview the exact stone slabs, furniture proportions, and daytime/evening lighting scenes before any construction begins.

Deliverables: 4K Photorealistic Renderings • Dimensioned Space Plans • Lighting Kelvin Lux Zoning
03
PHASE 03 • WEEKS 6 TO 8 Client Milestone: Material Swatches & Fixed BOQ

Refine • Material Procurement & Millwork Fabrication

We present physical stone slabs and timber veneers at the studio. Concurrently, technical fabrication drawings are issued to master European joiners and stone masons with zero ambiguity.

Deliverables: Physical Swatch Box • Millwork Shop Drawings • Guaranteed Fixed Cost Register
04
PHASE 04 • WEEKS 9 TO 14 Client Milestone: White-Glove Ceremonial Handover

Deliver • Turnkey Execution & Bespoke Styling

Our on-site project superintendent coordinates stone installation, modular cabinetry assembly, acoustic balancing, art hanging, deep cleaning, and the ceremonial project reveal.

Deliverables: On-Site White-Glove Handover • 10-Year Craft Warranty • Home Operation Manual
